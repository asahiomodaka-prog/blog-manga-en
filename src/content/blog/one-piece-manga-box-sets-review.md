## 1. Anatomy of the 4 Legendary Box Sets: From East Blue to the New World

Each *One Piece* box set is strategically engineered to correspond with major thematic eras of the grand saga.

### Box Set 1: East Blue and Baroque Works (Volumes 1–23)
The foundational era where legends are born. Beginning with Luffy’s humble departure in a wine barrel, this set chronicles the recruitment of Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji, culminating in the heart-wrenching Walk to Arlong Park. The saga then crosses into the Grand Line for the epic Arabasta saga, confronting Warlord Crocodile.
* **Key Arc Highlights**: Arlong Park, Loguetown, Drum Island (Chopper's backstory), Battle of Alubarna.
* **Bonus Inclusions**: Exclusive double-sided poster and the legendary 64-page mini-comic containing *Romance Dawn* (the prototype pilot that preceded One Piece).

### Box Set 2: Skypiea and Water Seven / Enies Lobby (Volumes 24–46)
Widely hailed by critics as the artistic zenith of shonen storytelling. The Straw Hats ascend 10,000 meters into the sky to battle God Enel, before plummeting into the corporate and political espionage of Water Seven. Robin’s tearful cry of *"I want to live!"* atop the Tower of Justice remains one of the most iconic moments in sequential art history.
* **Key Arc Highlights**: Jaya, Skypiea, Long Ring Long Land, Water Seven, Enies Lobby, Post-Enies Lobby.
* **Bonus Inclusions**: Full-color double-sided poster and an exclusive *One Piece* illustration gallery booklet.

### Box Set 3: Thriller Bark to the Summit War (Volumes 47–70)
The darkest, most operatic chapter in Luffy’s voyage. Transitioning from the gothic comedy of Thriller Bark into the brutal reality of the Sabaody Archipelago slave trade, Luffy’s crew is scattered across the globe by Bartholomew Kuma. Desperate to rescue his brother Ace, Luffy infiltrates the subterranean hell of Impel Down and storms Marineford in a war that fundamentally rewrites the geopolitical balance of the world.
* **Key Arc Highlights**: Thriller Bark (Brook's Binks' Sake), Sabaody Archipelago, Amazon Lily, Impel Down, Marineford Paramount War, Post-War Flashback, Return to Sabaody, Fishman Island.
* **Bonus Inclusions**: Exclusive collector poster and the historic *Cross Epoch* mini-comic—a collaborative crossover one-shot drawn by Akira Toriyama (*Dragon Ball*) and Eiichiro Oda.

### Box Set 4: Dressrosa, Zou, and Whole Cake Island (Volumes 71–90)
Entering the treacherous New World. The Straw Hats forge a pirate alliance with Trafalgar Law to dismantle Donquixote Doflamingo's sinister toy empire in Dressrosa. The journey then ascends the colossal ancient elephant Zou, leading into Sanji’s aristocratic family past in Whole Cake Island and a harrowing infiltration against Emperor Big Mom.
* **Key Arc Highlights**: Dressrosa, Zou, Whole Cake Island (Luffy vs Sanji, Katakuri duel), Reverie Council.
* **Bonus Inclusions**: Exclusive double-sided poster and a collector's mini-artbook featuring New World character sketches and promotional art.

## 4. Practical Collector Tips: Handling, Weight, and Display

Before receiving these hefty shipments, keep these practical home-gym-style collector considerations in mind:

1. **Substantial Physical Weight**: Each box set weighs approximately **10 to 12 pounds (4.5 to 5.5 kg)**. When all four sets arrive, you are handling nearly 45 pounds of solid paper and cardboard. Ensure your bookshelf tiers (such as IKEA Billy shelves) are rated for at least 30kg per shelf to prevent bowing over time.
2. **Dust and Sunlight Protection**: The rigid slipcases act as a natural protective armor against UV fading and ambient dust. Keeping your volumes stored inside the closed chests ensures the spine inks remain vibrant for decades, preserving resale and aesthetic value.
