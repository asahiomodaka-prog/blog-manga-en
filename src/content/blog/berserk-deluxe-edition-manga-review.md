## Beyond the Pages: Berserk's Legacy and Influence

*Berserk*'s influence extends far beyond the realm of manga. Its dark fantasy aesthetic, nuanced characters, and brutal themes have inspired countless video games (most notably the *Souls* series), anime adaptations (though none have quite captured the manga's magic), and even other Western fantasy works. Kentaro Miura's dedication to his craft, his relentless pursuit of artistic perfection, set a standard that few can hope to match.

Following Miura's tragic passing in 2021, the series is now being continued by his long-time assistant Kouji Mori and Studio Gaga, under the supervision of Miura's closest friends and colleagues. This continuation ensures that the saga of Guts and Casca will reach its intended conclusion, adding another layer of historical significance to every volume of the **Berserk Deluxe Edition Manga**.
